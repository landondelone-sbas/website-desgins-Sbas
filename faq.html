FAQ

Common questions

Anything we missed? Ask us on your discovery call.

What is an AI agent, and how is it different from an automation?

An automation follows the same steps every time. An agent handles work that involves reading, deciding or messy input, like emails and customer questions. On the discovery call we work out which one fits, so you do not pay for more than you need.

What happens on the discovery call?

We ask how your business runs, where your team's time goes and which tools you use. You do not need to prepare anything technical. Afterward we put together a plan for your business.

Do I need technical staff?

No. We build the agent, connect it to your tools, and show your team how to use it. We stay available as they get started.

What kinds of work can an agent take over?

Repeatable tasks like answering routine questions, sorting incoming requests, preparing reports, and moving information between systems.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the task. After the discovery call, your plan includes a timeline.

How does pricing work?

Every project is custom, so we quote after the discovery call, once we know what you need.

Is my business information safe?

We only connect to the systems an agent needs, and we agree with you up front on how your information is handled. See our privacy policy.

Will this replace my staff?

No. Agents take the repetitive parts, and your team keeps the judgment calls.
