Do I need an AI agent, or an automation?

Often a simple automation is enough. If the steps are always the same, software can just follow them. You need an agent when the work involves reading, deciding or handling messy input, like emails and customer questions. On the discovery call we work out which one fits, so you never pay for more than you need.
